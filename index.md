# TraceBin support

TraceBin turns one photo of a tool on a sheet of paper into a gridfinity bin, or a flat drawer insert, with a pocket cut to the tool's outline. It runs entirely on your iPhone.

## Getting a good trace

- Put one tool on a plain sheet of US Letter or A4 paper, on a surface that is not white.
- - Photograph from above with the whole sheet in frame. All four corners must be visible.
  - - Use the torch button in dim rooms. Diffuse daylight is best. Avoid hard shadows and strong reflections on chrome.
    - - If the outline misses part of a shiny tool, move the light or turn the tool over.
     
      - ## Fit
     
      - - 0.6 mm clearance suits most printers. Print the test coupon from Settings, then try a 20 mm block: loose means lower the clearance, tight means raise it.
        - - The pocket floor is 1.2 mm above the gridfinity base. Choose a fixed 12 or 8 mm depth if you want more material under the tool.
         
          - ## Files
         
          - - Exports are binary STL. They open in Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, and Cura with no repair needed.
            - - Bins follow the gridfinity spec: 42 mm grid, 7 mm height units, standard feet, 41.5 mm footprint per cell.
             
              - ## Known limits
             
              - One tool per photo. No finger notches, labels, or magnet holes yet. iPhone only, iOS 17 or later.
